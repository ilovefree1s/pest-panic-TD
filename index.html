            The bugs

              Good to know

              • Pick a tool in the tray to see its range circle, then place it on any empty floor tile beside the trail.
              • Your exterminator builds everything you order: he walks over, hammers it together, then moves on to the next job. Orders line up, so build before you send the next wave.
              • Don't like where he's standing? Press and drag him to any open tile. He'll wait there between jobs.
              • Backpack Spray (round button, bottom left, or press E): he sprints to the front of the bugs, turns around and fogs everything coming at him for 3 seconds from beside the trail. Costs 40 coins. Burns through shells and slows them. Recharges in 60 seconds.
              • Changed your mind? Anything you build or upgrade between waves sells back for the full price until the next wave starts.
              • Sound and music volume: tap the gear button (top right) for the Settings sliders.
              • Not sure what a tool does? Hold your finger on it in the tray for a second (or hold the mouse button) to see its full description.
              • Tools aim at the bug closest to the cake.
              • Candles stack, up to two. A tool standing in two Citronella Candle glows gets both boosts and shows two little flames. A third candle on the same tool adds nothing.
              • Tap a placed tool to upgrade it (twice) or sell it back for 60% of what you spent.
              • Every cleared wave pays a coin bonus. Squashed bugs drop coins too.
              • At the end of the trail the bugs climb the cabinet to reach the cake. Tools in the back right corner can still hit them there.
              • On a phone, play upright with the tools along the bottom, or turn it sideways for a bigger board with the tools on the right.
              • Coins don't have to go into tools. The Items tab has one-off buys: crumbs and traps for the trail, and a license that boosts every tool. Each item can be used once per wave.
              • Keys: 1–5 pick a tool, 6–9 pick an item, Space sends the next wave, F toggles 2× speed, P pauses, U upgrades, S sells, Esc cancels.
